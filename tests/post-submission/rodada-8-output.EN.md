## How to read this analysis:

**The code F-01, F-02…**
Every point in the analysis gets a fixed label. It serves one purpose: when you
send a new version of the proposal, the same point answers to the same code —
and you see at once what you resolved, what is half done and what did not change.

**Height** — from what distance the point was looked at:

- Height 1 = the letter of the call (rules, deadlines, the form).
- Height 2 = the quality of the writing (clarity and force of the text).
- Height 3 = strategy (what makes the proposal stronger in the scoring contest).

**Severity** — what ignoring the point costs:

- ELIMINATORY = may disqualify the proposal.
- COSTS POINTS = costs score on the evaluation criteria.
- SUGGESTION = strengthens the case; no direct cost in score.

**Confidence** — what backs the finding:

- CONFIRMED = the exact passage of the call was re-read this round and is cited alongside.
- TO BE CHECKED = it could not be confirmed in the text of the call — confirm with the Secretaria's mediators before acting.

**YOUR CALL**
When this marker appears, the point is not an error in your text: the call
requires a choice only you can make. The analysis shows what is at stake in
each option; the choice is yours.

## 1. Findings — detailed

**[F-15]** · Height 1: Conformity with the call · Severity: **ELIMINATORY** · Confidence: CONFIRMED · **YOUR CALL**

- Passage from the proposal: *"60% of its fixed core of artists (R. and G.) and the guest guitarist reside and build their careers in the municipality of Hortolândia/SP, while the remaining roles extend to Campinas and Monte Mor."*
- Anchor in the call: item 3.1 — *"Any cultural agent may enter this call, an Individual or a Legal Entity (of predominantly cultural nature): […] 3.1.2. resident for at least 02 (two) years in the municipality of Campinas, in the case of an Individual"*. The mechanism that bars comes next: Anexo VIII, 3.1.3–3.1.4, requires a recent proof of residence **and** one from at least 24 months before the call's publication — without them there is no qualification.
- Why it matters: the Campinas requirement applies **only to the applicant** — the team may be from any city, with no limit (help-desk Q&A, "A exigência de morar em Campinas vale para quem?…", 15/07/26, 10:27:09 · 15/07/26, 10:28:36). So the problem is not the team living elsewhere. The problem is that the proposal, as written, places two of the three core members in Hortolândia without saying who the applicant cultural agent is. **If the applicant is R. or G., the entry does not stand** — and that is not one point less, it is the end of the project at the qualification stage. I do not know who signs the entry; you do, and checking takes ten seconds.
- What to do: confirm now who the applicant is and whether she holds the two Campinas proofs (the recent one and the 24+ months one). If all is well, what remains is a writing decision: as it stands, the text hands the reviewer the impression of a mostly-Hortolândia project, without saying that the applicant is from Campinas.

**[F-13]** · Height 1: Conformity with the call · Severity: **COSTS POINTS** · Confidence: CONFIRMED

- Passage from the proposal: *"01 (one) free directed open rehearsal aimed at the local community"* (field 2.2) and *"the in-person offering of the directed open rehearsal for the local community in the Metropolitan Region of Campinas"* (field 3.2).
- Anchor in the call: item 7.6 — *"In the Access and Accessibility Plan, the cultural agent must provide for the adoption of at least one measure of democratization of access"*, and 7.6.3 — *"holding activities parallel to the projects free of charge, such as open rehearsals, courses, training, lectures, exhibitions, showings and workshops"*. And it appears twice as a number and not once as an action: field 3.1 (description of the Plan) does not mention it; field 3.3 answers only "digital music streaming platforms and cloud repository", with no physical venue, no date, no time; and the schedule in 4.1 has no rehearsal in any of the six months. If you do not want to open that flank three hours from the deadline, the alternative is to describe the rehearsal as a free parallel activity (7.6.3, which is already enough) and not as training.

**[F-14]** · Height 3: Strategy · Severity: **COSTS POINTS** · Confidence: CONFIRMED

An attentive reviewer reads a project that records in Campinas but presents itself as being from Hortolândia, mixes in Belo Horizonte, and calls that decentralization of Campinas. And there is an arithmetic inconsistency in plain sight: R. and G. are 2 of 3 of the "fixed core" (67%), not 60% — the 60% only works if you count the guest, who is not fixed core.
